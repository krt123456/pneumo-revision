# IELTS research (checked 22 Nov 2026)

## 1. Academic format
- Listening: 30 min, 40 questions, four parts (two monologues, two conversations). https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/ielts-test-format
- Reading: 60 min, 40 questions, three passages. https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/ielts-test-format
- Writing: 60 min, 2 tasks (academic report, essay). https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/ielts-test-format
- Speaking: 11-14 min, three parts; may fall on the same day as other sections or within 7 days. https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/ielts-test-format
- Format change for 2025-2026: the British Council format page announces none.
- Delivery change (announced 5 Mar 2026): paper-based IELTS is being discontinued from about mid-2026, with computer delivery as the only mode. A "Writing on Paper" option (handwritten Writing only) appears in selected markets. The announcement says the skills assessed and scoring are unchanged. Market timing varies; no country dates named. https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/updates-to-ielts-test-delivery
- ielts.org test-format page: fetch failed (client error). NOT FOUND there.
